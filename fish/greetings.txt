# Old English phrases for the terminal greeting (fish/functions/dungeon_greeting.fish).
# One per line: Old English <TAB> modern English. Type them in plain letters (þ ð æ and
# macrons like ā are fine); the greeting turns them into fraktur and w into wynn (ƿ).
Wes hāl	Be well
Hwæt!	Listen!
Wyrd bið ful āræd	Fate is wholly fixed
Gǣð ā wyrd swā hīo scel	Fate always goes as it must
Þæs oferēode, þisses swā mæg	That passed away, so may this
Þæt wæs gōd cyning!	That was a good king!
Bēowulf is mīn nama	Beowulf is my name
Ofer hronrāde	Over the whale-road
Hāl wes þū, folde, fīra mōdor	Hail to you, earth, mother of men
Ēalā Ēarendel, engla beorhtast	Hail Earendel, brightest of angels
Wyrce sē þe mōte dōmes ǣr dēaþe	Let him who may win glory before death
Hige sceal þē heardra, heorte þē cēnre	The will must be the firmer, the heart the keener
Mōd sceal þē māre, þē ūre mægen lȳtlað	Courage must be the greater as our strength wanes
Eall þis eorþan gesteal īdel weorþeð	All this world's foundation will lie waste
